## 6. How to Follow Along

This repository is intended to be useful not only as my personal learning log but also as a reference for other students beginning their Data Analytics journey.

If you want to follow a similar path:

1. Start with the foundational topics and follow the roadmap in order.
2. Explore the daily learning notes and practical exercises.
3. Work through the datasets independently before checking any solutions.
4. Attempt the exercises rather than copying code.
5. Review the project documentation and understand the reasoning behind each analysis.
6. Revisit difficult concepts instead of rushing ahead.
7. Build your own projects using different datasets and business questions.

**Important:** This repository documents my learning process. It is not intended to be a ready-made course or a collection of solutions to copy. Use it to learn, experiment, question, and build independently.

## 7. Learning Principles

### Mastery Before Schedule

Completing a day does not automatically mean mastering its topic. Progress depends on whether I can independently apply what I have learned.

### Attempt Before Solution

I will attempt exercises and solve problems before consulting complete solutions. Hints and guided reasoning come first.

### Understand the Data Before Analyzing It

Every project begins with understanding the dataset, its structure, its limitations, and the questions it can answer.

### Always Ask "So What?"

An analysis is useful only when its findings have meaning.

My approach:

**Data → Finding → Meaning → Decision**

### Quality Over Quantity

A few well-documented, reproducible, meaningful projects are more valuable than numerous copied tutorial projects.

### Responsible Analytics

I will consider data quality, bias, privacy, uncertainty, correlation versus causation, and the limitations of analytical methods.

### Build Independence

The ultimate goal is to solve unfamiliar analytical problems independently, not become dependent on AI-generated answers.

## 8. Transparency and Mistakes Log

Learning involves mistakes, incorrect assumptions, failed approaches, and revisions. This repository will document those experiences instead of presenting only polished results.

The purpose of this section is to make the learning process transparent and show how mistakes contribute to improvement.

### What I Will Document

- Incorrect assumptions or misunderstandings.
- Bugs, coding errors, and implementation issues.
- Data-cleaning mistakes and overlooked data-quality problems.
- Analytical approaches that did not work as expected.
- Incorrect interpretations or unsupported conclusions.
- Changes to project methodology and the reasons behind them.
- Lessons learned and steps taken to prevent similar mistakes.

### Mistakes Log

| Date | Topic / Project | Mistake or Issue | Correction / Lesson | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | — | No entries yet. | Entries will be added as issues arise. | — |

### Transparency Commitments

1. I will not hide meaningful mistakes simply because they make the work look less polished.
2. I will explain important changes to methodology and analytical decisions.
3. I will distinguish between assumptions, observations, and conclusions.
4. I will correct errors when discovered and document significant corrections.
5. I will not claim a project, skill, or result is complete without supporting work.

The goal is not to avoid mistakes. It is to recognize them, understand why they happened, correct them, and improve.

### Dataset Licensing and Attribution

Datasets used in this repository will be obtained from public or otherwise authorized sources.

For each dataset, I will aim to document:

- Original source
- Dataset description
- License and usage restrictions
- Attribution requirements
- Modifications made during cleaning
- Whether the data is real or synthetic

Publicly accessible does not automatically mean unrestricted for redistribution. Original dataset licenses and terms will be respected. Where redistribution is not permitted, I will provide source links and instructions for obtaining the data instead of uploading the dataset.
